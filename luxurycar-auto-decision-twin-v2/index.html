DEALER-OWNED PRIVATE INVENTORY NETWORK

Why limit your showroom to the cars you own?

Haraj helps advertise cars that are already for sale. This prototype tests a different model: keep past buyers, wanted-car requests and opt-in owner garages inside the showroom's own network—so a deal can begin before a car is publicly listed.

THE CORE ECONOMIC IDEA

Sold customers become future supply.

A car sold today can stay in the customer's private garage. Later, the owner can quietly open it to serious offers. The showroom can match that car to a waiting buyer without first owning the vehicle.

SYSTEM BOUNDARY

What this prototype is — and is not.

1 · InventoryShowroom-owned vehicles remain normal stock.
2 · GarageCustomer vehicles stay private unless the owner opts in.
3 · WantedBuyers specify exactly what they want.
4 · MatchSoftware surfaces opportunities; humans qualify them.
5 · Legal transactionBrokerage, consignment, payment and transfer use the showroom's lawful licensed process.
Working interactive prototype by Digital Arhat. Not officially affiliated with Luxurycar_USA. Only the linked public listing is presented as prospect-sourced factual inventory. Private-owner, wanted-car, dashboard and network counts are clearly simulated to demonstrate the proposed operating model. No real customer identity, private vehicle, payment, brokerage, auction or escrow activity is represented.
